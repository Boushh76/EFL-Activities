It or there?

In Norwegian we say det er for both. In English we must choose.

The rule

there is / there are Something exists or is in a place.
You can say det finnes or det eksisterer.

There is a plant in the fridge.
= Det finnes en plante i kjøleskapet.
it is It points to one thing we already know about. We also use it for weather, time and how something is.

The plant is in a boot. It is green.
= Den er grønn.
It is cold. It is late.

The test: Try to say «det finnes» or «det eksisterer».
It makes sense → write there. It does not make sense → write it.
Remember: there is + one thing, there are + many things.
